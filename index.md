# HUSH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

HUSH ("the game") is a co-op climbing game for phones. This page explains what happens to your data when you play.

## What stays on your phone
Your progress, the look you wear and the items you bought are saved only on your phone. Progress means the badges you earned, the journal pages you found and the titans you climbed. None of it is ever sent to us.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it. Purchases can be restored from the store.

## Playing online
To play with friends, the game connects to Photon Cloud, run by Exit Games GmbH. Photon's servers in Japan pass the game between the players in a room: the climbers' moves, the room code and the items.

As with any internet service, Photon's servers see your device's IP address while you're connected. The game sends Photon no name, contacts, location or account.

Photon's privacy policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

## Purchases
Purchases go through Google Play (on iPhone, the App Store). The store handles your payment, and we never see your payment details. The game only learns which items you own, and keeps that list on your phone.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What we don't do
- No accounts, and no sign-in.
- No ads, no advertising ID, and no tracking.
- We don't collect personal data, and we don't sell or share it.
- The Share button opens your phone's own share sheet. You choose what to share, and with whom.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13.

## The tools the game is made with
The game is made with Unity. Unity's in-app purchasing library handles store purchases on your phone.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Changes and contact
