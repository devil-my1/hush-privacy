# HUSH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

HUSH ("the game") is a co-op climbing game for phones. This page explains what happens to your data when you play.

## What stays on your phone
Your progress, the look you wear, the items you bought, your best times and your settings (the sound's volume, and the graphics level the game picked for your phone or you chose) are saved on your phone. Progress means the badges you earned, the journal pages you found and the titans you climbed. So are your friends' anonymous IDs, a friend's time to beat, and the anonymous IDs of players you muted (below). None of it is sent to us, except a gem's time for the leaderboards (below).

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it from the phone. If your phone's own backup is on (Google on Android, iCloud on iPhone), a copy may stay in your backup account, under your control, and can come back when you reinstall. Purchases can be restored from the store.

## Playing online
To play with friends, the game connects to Photon Cloud, run by Exit Games GmbH. Photon's servers in Japan pass the game between the players in a room: the climbers' moves, the room code, the items, and each player's anonymous ID (for friends, below).

As with any internet service, Photon's servers see your device's IP address while you're connected. The game sends Photon no name, contacts, location or account.

Photon's privacy policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

## Leaderboards, statistics and crash reports
The game uses Google's Firebase for its leaderboards, for statistics about how the game is played, and for crash reports.

- **An anonymous ID.** The game gives your install a random ID. It has no name, email or sign-in behind it, and uninstalling the game loses it.
- **Leaderboards.** When you take a titan's gem, the game sends your ID, a name made from it (like "Climber 4F2A"), your time and your noise percent. Every player sees that name, time and noise on the titan's leaderboards.
- **Statistics.** Each climb sends one event: the titan, your time, your falls, your noise, whether you took the gem, and whether a friend was there. Every five minutes of play, the game also sends how smoothly it ran: the frame rate, the graphics level, the memory it used and how much the phone has, the battery's drain, how warm the phone got, how many climbers were in the room, and how much of the time you were on the titan. Firebase also counts, on its own, when the game is first opened, how long it's played, the game's version, the phone's model and system, and the country (from the IP address).
- **Crash reports.** If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends what went wrong, with the phone's model and system.

All of it is tied to the anonymous ID, never to you by name. The game collects no advertising ID. Google stores this data for the game: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

To have your leaderboard entries removed, email us (below) with your Climber name.

## Friends, "Beat my time" links and clips
- **Friends.** When you play in a room, the phones there swap the anonymous IDs behind the leaderboards. Your phone keeps the IDs of the 29 players it met most recently, in rooms or through their links. To show their times on a friends' leaderboard, the game sends those IDs to Firebase and reads their entries on the leaderboards above; the list itself stays on your phone.
- **Muting.** A tap beside a room-mate's card mutes their pings and emotes on your phone. Your phone keeps the anonymous IDs of the 50 players you muted most recently, so they stay muted when you meet them again. The list is never sent to us or to other players.
- **"Beat my time" links.** After you take a gem, Share can add a link to your card: it carries the titan, your time and your anonymous ID, so a friend who opens it sees your time to beat and adds you as a friend. You choose whether to share it, and with whom.
- **The link's page.** A link opens a small page at hush-5c70b.web.app, served by Google's Firebase Hosting. The page reads the link in your browser, loads nothing from other sites, and sends us nothing; as with any website, Google's servers see your IP address when it loads.
- **Pictures and clips (Android).** Photo mode saves your pictures to your gallery (Pictures/HUSH). "Share the fall" makes a short GIF of your last 8 seconds on your phone and saves it there too. They go only where you share them.

## Purchases
Purchases go through Google Play (on iPhone, the App Store). The store handles your payment, and we never see your payment details. The game only learns which items you own, and keeps that list on your phone.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## What we don't do
- No accounts to make, and no sign-in.
- No chat or voice: players in a room can only send a few fixed signals.
- No access to your microphone, camera, contacts or location.
- No ads, no advertising ID, and no tracking across other apps.
- We don't collect your name, email, contacts or location, and we don't sell or share your data.
- The Share and "Share the fall" buttons open your phone's own share sheet. You choose what to share, and with whom.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13.

## The tools the game is made with
The game is made with Unity. Unity's in-app purchasing library handles store purchases on your phone.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
