# HUSH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

HUSH ("the game") is a co-op climbing game for phones. This page explains what happens to your data when you play.

## What stays on your phone
Your progress, the look you wear, the items you bought and your best times are saved on your phone. Progress means the badges you earned, the journal pages you found and the titans you climbed. None of it is sent to us, except a summit's time for the leaderboards (below).

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it. Purchases can be restored from the store.

## Playing online
To play with friends, the game connects to Photon Cloud, run by Exit Games GmbH. Photon's servers in Japan pass the game between the players in a room: the climbers' moves, the room code and the items.

As with any internet service, Photon's servers see your device's IP address while you're connected. The game sends Photon no name, contacts, location or account.

Photon's privacy policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

## Leaderboards, statistics and crash reports
The game uses Google's Firebase for its leaderboards, for statistics about how the game is played, and for crash reports.

- **An anonymous ID.** The game gives your install a random ID. It has no name, email or sign-in behind it, and uninstalling the game loses it.
- **Leaderboards.** When you reach a titan's summit, the game sends your ID, a name made from it (like "Climber 4F2A"), your time and your noise percent. Every player sees that name, time and noise on the titan's leaderboards.
- **Statistics.** Each climb sends one event: the titan, your time, your falls, your noise, whether you reached the summit, and whether a friend was there. Firebase also counts, on its own, when the game is first opened, how long it's played, the game's version, the phone's model and system, and the country (from the IP address).
- **Crash reports.** If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends what went wrong, with the phone's model and system.

All of it is tied to the anonymous ID, never to you by name. The game collects no advertising ID. Google stores this data for the game: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

To have your leaderboard entries removed, open an issue (below) with your Climber name.

## Purchases
Purchases go through Google Play (on iPhone, the App Store). The store handles your payment, and we never see your payment details. The game only learns which items you own, and keeps that list on your phone.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What we don't do
- No accounts to make, and no sign-in.
- No ads, no advertising ID, and no tracking across other apps.
- We don't collect your name, email, contacts or location, and we don't sell or share your data.
- The Share button opens your phone's own share sheet. You choose what to share, and with whom.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13.

## The tools the game is made with
The game is made with Unity. Unity's in-app purchasing library handles store purchases on your phone.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
